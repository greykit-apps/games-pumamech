# PumaMech

**The Sunclaws Rise.** A remake of *BattleTech: The Crescent Hawk's Inception* (Westwood Associates for Infocom, 1988; Commodore 64, 1989): a puma cadet, a borrowed training mech, and an invasion. The world, the mechs, the people and every word are the game's own; only the shape of the game is the 1988 one.

**Play:** [greykit.com/games/pumamech](https://greykit.com/games/pumamech/)

![The share card: a lance of three walking in on the road west of Portgate's bridge](og.png)

## How it plays

You are a cadet at the **Bastion**, the mech academy of Pardalis. Take lessons in the Training Hall, then fly three sorties in your **Tabby** trainer; the third is the graduation run, and the morning the **Grey Pact** lands. After that the world is occupied: walk it a tile at a time through four towns with inns, shops, a mech yard, a clinic and a bank with a **stock exchange**, gather a lance of misfits, and find the **Founders' Vault** in the High Spurs, sealed by red, blue and yellow codes.

Fights are **rounds of orders**, as in 1988: plot each unit's route and a target for each weapon, then the round plays out with everyone moving at once. Mechs are hit by **location**, build **heat**, and can be **salvaged** if beaten without being blown apart.

## Keys

| | |
|---|---|
| Arrows, WASD | walk; in a fight, move the cursor |
| `Enter`, `Space` | talk, choose; in a fight, confirm |
| `P` `J` `Tab` | the party, the journal, the map |
| `Esc` | the menu; in a fight, back a step |
| `M` | sound |

The fight keys are listed on screen. The mouse and a gamepad work too. A desktop game: on a phone or a small window the page says so instead.

## Saves

Kept in the browser's `localStorage` under keys starting `pumamech_`: the autosave, three slots, settings and sound. **Settings → Forget everything** clears them.
